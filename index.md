I’m **Muhammad Riduwan Hafidi**, a 18-year-old student focused on building **developer tools, libraries, and CLI utilities**.

My work emphasizes **clarity over cleverness**. I care about software that is easy to reason about, predictable in behavior, and practical to maintain over time.

## Principles

Good tools should reduce cognitive load, not increase it.

### Design Values
- Minimal surface area
- Explicit configuration
- Predictable behavior
- Early and clear failure modes
